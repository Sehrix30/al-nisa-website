Evidence of Work. Relationships That Strengthen It.

Al-Nisa Welfare Society works in Okara through community programs, institutional coordination, and partnerships with government departments, academic institutions, development organizations, and local welfare networks.They help us connect people facing social or financial barriers with organizations and services that can assist them.

Our Impact at a Glance

A measurable view of the work carried out through Al-Nisa's healthcare and community programs.

200+
Free Medical Camps

Community-based medical camps providing healthcare access to people who may face financial or social barriers to medical care.

50%
Treatment Subsidy

Eligible marginalized patients referred through Al-Nisa’s healthcare network can access treatment with a 50% subsidy at partner hospitals.

5
Partner Hospitals

Five major hospitals in Okara are part of Al-Nisa’s healthcare referral network for subsidized treatment.

40,000+
Beneficiaries Reached

Partner & Collaboration Matrix

Al-Nisa works with organizations across international development, government, and academic institutions.

UNICEF Pakistan

Al-Nisa Welfare Society has collaborated with UNICEF Pakistan on initiatives related to child protection, prevention of child abuse, and efforts addressing child begging.The Society’s involvement in this area supports community awareness around children’s rights, safety, and protection.

International & NGO

The Asia Foundation

Al-Nisa works with The Asia Foundation in areas connected with gender-based violence, public health, and community awareness.This work relates to issues affecting women’s safety, health, and access to support within the community.

What Our Partnerships Make Possible

Six operational areas strengthened through institutional collaboration.

Healthcare Coordination

Relationships with district health institutions and hospitals help connect vulnerable patients with healthcare services and subsidized treatment.

Child & Women Protection

Collaboration with development organizations and law-enforcement institutions supports work related to child protection, GBV, victim support

Poverty Reduction

The collaboration with UAF connects Al-Nisa’s community experience with institutional efforts focused on poverty reduction and local development.

Rehabilitation

Work with the District Probation and Parole Service includes awareness activities, conduct sessions, and rehabilitation-related efforts among probationers in District Okara.

Civic Awareness

Engagement with the Election Commission of Pakistan supports voter awareness, identity documentation, and greater understanding of civic participation.

Institutional Accountability

Registration and coordination with government institutions provide an important institutional framework for Al-Nisa’s community work.
